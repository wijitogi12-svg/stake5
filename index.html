Mission

Yearnsfi explains staking security as part of the strategy.

For us, strong staking content must answer not only the question “how much can you earn,” but also “what can go wrong.” That is why the site includes breakdowns of slashing, delegation, liquidity, custody models, and informational pages for users who need not a marketing slogan but a clear decision-making framework.

What we consider essential before staking

  • Check the network rules and unbonding periods instead of relying only on an attractive yield figure.
  • Evaluate the validator by uptime, penalty history, communication, and the quality of its operational infrastructure.
  • Understand how the risk changes if liquid staking or a service model is used instead of native staking.
