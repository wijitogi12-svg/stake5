Editorial and Support

For content questions, publication corrections, fact checks, and requests for new topics, write to hello@yearnsfi.com. If you need a quick route through the site’s guide section, we also answer at support@yearnsfi.com and help match materials to your level of experience.

Partnerships and Media

For joint research, interviews, sponsored reviews, and expert commentary, use partnerships@yearnsfi.com. We only consider proposals where the educational value for the audience clearly outweighs the promotional component and where the partner’s role can be disclosed transparently.

Communications

We maintain a presence on the main channels used to share concise market updates and announcements of new articles. This page gathers active contact details, including the editorial phone line and social links, so users can choose the communication method they prefer and get a faster response.

Response Time

We usually handle editorial requests within one business day. Reports of inaccuracies in guides and questions about site navigation are prioritized because the quality and timeliness of educational materials depend on them.

What to Prepare Before Reaching Out

If you are contacting us about a specific network, validator, or liquid staking product, include a link to the material, briefly describe your question, and note whether you need a review, commentary, a data correction, or a partnership discussion. That helps the team route your request to the right specialist more quickly.
