Privacy

Yearnsfi publishes educational and analytical content, so we aim to collect as little data as possible and use it only to respond to user requests, analyze content quality, and improve site navigation. On this page we explain what information may reach us, how it is used, and what rights remain with the visitor.

Guide

Privacy

Yearnsfi privacy policy for readers and partners.

What Data May Be Processed

If a user writes to the editorial team, we receive only the information they send themselves: name, email address, message text, and, when needed, contact details for a reply. The site may also collect anonymized page-view statistics so we can understand which topics interest the audience and where the content structure should be improved.

How We Use Information

Contact details are used only to respond to an inquiry, discuss a partnership, or clarify an editorial question. Anonymized analytics help us improve navigation, card layouts, related-material recommendations, and publishing frequency. We do not treat user data as a product, and we do not build aggressive monetization models around personal information.

User Rights

A visitor may request that the contact information they provided be updated or deleted, and may also ask how it was used. When the matter concerns editorial correspondence, we keep that communication only for as long as necessary to respond, follow up on the topic, or fulfill agreed obligations related to a partnership request.

Legal Contact

If you have a request related to privacy, rights to materials, terms of use, or the accuracy of published information, please write to legal@yearnsfi.com. We handle these messages separately from editorial email so we can respond faster and keep legal matters distinct from content support.
